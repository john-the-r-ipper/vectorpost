A growing share of online shopping does not happen on a traditional storefront. It happens inside a chat thread. In markets where Messenger and WhatsApp are the default way to talk to a business, the "storefront" is often just a conversation. The question for a merchant is not whether to show up there, but how to run the whole transaction without drowning in manual replies.

OnlyOrders is a platform built for that specific problem. Its tagline is "Your orders, on autopilot," and the product pitch is direct: "OnlyOrders turns DMs and comments into confirmed orders — smart replies, live catalog checks, payment verification and courier handoff, all from one inbox." This post explains what that means and where the product sits in the commerce stack.

## The category: conversational commerce

OnlyOrders describes itself as a "conversational commerce" tool — "the AI copilot for social sellers, from the first salaam to the five-star review." The category is about selling through messaging rather than through a checkout page. Orders begin with a question in a DM, a comment on a post, or a product photo someone sends over.

The core idea is that the message thread is not a lead-generation channel that eventually points to a website. It *is* the place where the sale closes. That puts a different set of demands on merchants: they need to answer quickly, check stock, confirm details, take payment, and arrange delivery — all while keeping the buyer in the conversation.

## What the product offers

The OnlyOrders homepage is built around four feature blocks:

- **Instant replies** — "AI answers customers 24/7."
- **One inbox** — "Every channel, zero tab chaos."
- **Chat to order** — "Receipts → courier handoff."
- **Auto posts** — "Drops published on schedule."

The hero summarizes the whole idea: "Everything a chat-first store needs, in one place." The merchant gets a single inbox spanning channels, an AI that answers routine questions in the shop's tone, and a pipeline that carries a conversation from first message through to delivery.

## Why this matters for content teams

From a content-operations perspective, conversational commerce is a useful case study. The AI replies are only as good as the product information behind them. A catalog that is structured, current, and machine-readable is what lets an assistant answer a question like "Is this in stock?" correctly. The content model and the sales model become the same model, as discussed in our post on [publishing workflows that move orders](/blog/publishing-workflows-orders/).

OnlyOrders is available at [onlyorders.tech](https://onlyorders.tech). The product offers a free plan for trying it out, with paid tiers for growing stores — which we cover in our [OnlyOrders hub](/onlyorders/).

## Bottom line

OnlyOrders is a conversational-commerce platform for social sellers: it handles AI auto-replies, inbox management, chat-based checkout, order dashboards, and courier handoff from one place. Its positioning is not about replacing a website so much as meeting customers where they already are — in the chat.
