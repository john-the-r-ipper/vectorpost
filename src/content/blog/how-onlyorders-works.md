Understanding how a tool works is the fastest way to see whether it fits your operation. OnlyOrders is a conversational-commerce platform, so its core flow is a message thread with a beginning, a middle, and a delivery. This post walks through the four stages of that flow as published on the product site.

## The four-stage loop

The product describes itself as running "from first message to delivered order," in four steps:

1. **Messenger AI replies (Active)** — answers price, size, and stock questions in seconds, in the shop's tone, and hands off when it matters.
2. **Checkout inside the chat** — the customer confirms the cart, address, and payment without leaving Messenger.
3. **One orders dashboard** — every chat order lands in a single board with status, notes, and totals.
4. **Courier tracking, auto-shared** — book the courier in one tap; tracking updates are sent back to the buyer's chat.

## Step 1: AI replies

The conversation opens with a customer message. The AI responds instantly, in the merchant's voice, answering the questions that are routine: price, size, stock, availability. When the conversation becomes complex or sensitive, the system hands off to a human. The point of this step is to never leave the customer waiting at the exact moment their intent is highest.

## Step 2: Checkout in the chat

Rather than redirecting the buyer to an external checkout, the order is confirmed inside the thread. The customer verifies the cart line items, the delivery address, and the payment method without switching contexts. In the demo, this happens as a plain exchange: "Yes please, cash on delivery." → "Done. Order #OO-2481 is confirmed." The friction of leaving the chat is removed.

## Step 3: One orders dashboard

Every order that closes in a conversation lands in a single board. The dashboard gives the merchant a view of status, notes, and totals across all channels — a counterweight to the "tab chaos" of juggling several inboxes. It is the operational hub where the merchant actually runs the day.

## Step 4: Courier tracking, auto-shared

The final leg is logistics. The merchant books a courier in one tap, and the tracking updates are sent back automatically into the buyer's chat. The customer never has to ask "where is my order?" — the status arrives in the same thread where they bought. This closes the loop and sets up the next step: a review.

## Why the loop matters

Each stage feeds the next. A good AI reply keeps the buyer in the thread; in-chat checkout captures the order; the dashboard keeps it organized; and shared tracking keeps the buyer informed. From a content perspective, the whole flow depends on accurate product facts being available to the assistant — the same structured content we write about across this blog.
